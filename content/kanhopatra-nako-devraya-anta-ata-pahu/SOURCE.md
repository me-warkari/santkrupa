- Fix 2026-11-24: verse १ read "प्राण हा सवर्था" on Wikisource; every witness reads "सर्वथा" (the printed booklet, the blogspot gatha, esamskriti) — "सवर्था" is not a word. Fixed to "सर्वथा" (1 occurrence).
- Noted variant, kept as-is: verse १ second half reads "जाऊ पाहे" here (Wikisource; also esamskriti), while the printed booklet and the blogspot gatha read "फुटो पाहे" — a genuine variant in the tradition, not a certain error.
