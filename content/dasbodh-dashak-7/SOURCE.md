No Latin-letter junk remains in the file. The S4 "gap 53–91" and S9 "gap 61–98" are phantom numbering jumps — both samas end where the print ends them (52 and 60). The comparison witness merges ovis in four places (S1 10+11, S2 31+32, S7 51+52, S9 22+23) and drops S3 ovi 19, which is in our scan and in the ITRANS transcription, so it is genuine and kept. The witness also inserts one extra Sanskrit sloka before S10 ovi 41 (यस्य देवे पराभक्तिर्यथा देवे तथा गुरौ) that is in neither our scan nor the ITRANS transcription — it was dropped, not added.
