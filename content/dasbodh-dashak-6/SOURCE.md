No Latin-letter junk remains in the file. The comparison witness merges no ovis here, but it inserts one extra Sanskrit sloka in S6 (नानाशास्त्रविदो…) that is in neither our scan nor the ITRANS transcription — it was dropped, not added. The witness also lacks the अश्वत्थः sloka-pada line, which our scan and the transcription both carry.
